*Delivery involved 20 MW of capacity, two COLO environments, and a large-scale telecommunications infrastructure.*

High-complexity Data Center projects require a high level of precision, coordination, and reliability, especially when involving mission-critical environments. It was in this context that Consultimer acted in the execution of a large-scale project, successfully completed.
From the beginning, the focus was on project governance, schedule predictability, and technical excellence, ensuring a consistent delivery aligned with industry best practices.

**Scope:** Large-scale critical infrastructure.

The project included the complete implementation of the telecommunications infrastructure of a Data Center with **20 MW of total capacity**, distributed across **two COLO environments**, as well as a robust network infrastructure.

The complexity of the scope required structured planning, integration between multiple work fronts, and continuous execution monitoring, ensuring adherence to the technical and operational standards expected for this type of environment.

### Management and execution: coordination, scale, and technical precision

Consultimer adopted a management model oriented towards critical milestones, with integrated action between technical teams, field coordination, and the involved stakeholders, ensuring execution fluidity and agile decision-making throughout the entire project.

The scale of the operation is evidenced by the main delivery numbers:

- **328 racks assembled and positioned;**
- **More than 10 km of optical fibers installed;**
- **More than 500 fusions performed;**
- **More than 2 km of UTP cables deployed;**
- **More than 1,000 network points delivered.**

The technical execution relied on a multidisciplinary team, combining experienced professionals and developing talents, promoting standardization, quality, and continuous evolution of internal competencies.

### Results: consistent delivery and institutional strengthening

The project was completed within the planned timeframe, meeting the technical, operational, and quality requirements expected for a high-complexity Data Center.

Among the main results, the following stand out:

- Structured and reliable infrastructure delivery;
- High level of organization and execution control;
- Expansion of Consultimer's expertise in mission-critical projects;
- Operational efficiency gains;
- Strengthening of the company's positioning in the global market.

The operation featured a scalable team, starting with a small group and reaching about 40 professionals during peak intensity periods, always acting in a coordinated manner and aligned with the project's objectives.

### Consultimer Differential: specialization in critical environments

The delivery reinforces Consultimer's positioning as a company specialized in critical infrastructures and high-complexity projects, with consistent operations in Latin America.

Its differential lies in the combination of technical competence, project governance, transparent communication, and a focus on the customer experience, ensuring reliable deliveries even in large-scale and highly critical scenarios.

### A milestone in Consultimer's trajectory

More than a technical delivery, the project represents an institutional milestone, evidencing Consultimer's maturity in managing complex projects with high standards of quality, organization, and responsibility.
